SVD is built from the diagonalization of two symmetric matrices:



**Right side (input space):**

$$
A^T A = V \Sigma^2 V^T
$$


1. Eigenvectors: right singular vectors $v_i$
2. Eigenvalues: $\lambda_i = \sigma_i^2$


**Left side (output space):**

$$
A A^T = U \Sigma^2 U^T
$$


1. Eigenvectors: left singular vectors $u_i$
2. Eigenvalues: $\lambda_i = \sigma_i^2$
