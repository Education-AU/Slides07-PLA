SVD plays the same geometric role as diagonalization: it expresses a linear map in orthonormal coordinates where it acts purely by scaling.

It applies to any linear map $L:V\rightarrow W$ between finite-dimensional inner product spaces, where $\dim(V)=n$ and $\dim(W)=m$.

It is derived from the spectral theorem.

We first state the theorem, then derive it.
