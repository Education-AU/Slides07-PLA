To better understand the theorem, we go through its proof, which is based on the spectral theorem.

We work in matrix coordinates, considering
$$
A : \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^m.
$$

A key idea is to study the matrix
$$
A^T A : \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^n,
$$
which allows us to apply the spectral theorem.

We first establish some important properties of $A^T A$ and $AA^T$.
